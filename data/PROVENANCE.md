## Presentation-layer addition (NOT part of frozen V1)

`data/city_coordinates.csv` — lat/lon for map positioning only. The frozen V1
exports contain no coordinates, and the prototype brief requires a map.

- Source: GeoNames `cities15000` (CC BY 4.0), downloaded 2026-09-11 from
  <https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip> (kept in `assets/raw/`).
- Built by `analysis/build_city_coordinates.py` (deterministic; report:
  `analysis/coordinate_match_report.md`). 391/391 explorer cities resolved to a
  real GeoNames place: 357 by full name, 20 by comma/slash/compound name parts,
  14 by documented principal-city alias (FUAs named after regions/districts,
  e.g. "Ruhrgebiet"→Dortmund, "West Midlands urban area"→Birmingham).
  Country-code mapping for the match only: UK→GB, EL→GR.
- Coordinates position dots ONLY. Every displayed value (population, raw
  indicators, percentile scores, years, staleness) comes from the frozen CSVs.
- Known positioning approximations are listed per-row in `match_field` and in the
  match report; Fort-de-France (FR, Martinique) lies outside the initial Europe
  frame — the map pans/zooms to any selected city.
